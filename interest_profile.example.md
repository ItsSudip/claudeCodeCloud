# Interest Profile

## About Me
I'm a software engineer and startup founder. I build data infrastructure and developer tools,
and I'm hands-on with LLM-based products. I read to make better technical and business
decisions, not to be entertained. I prefer primary sources, benchmarks, post-mortems and
first-hand founder write-ups over hot takes.

## Themes

### Tech Breakthroughs (weight: 1.0)
keywords: llm, language model, open-weight, open source, benchmark, inference, compiler, rust, wasm, database, postgres, distributed systems, observability, tracing, kubernetes, performance, paper, arxiv, agents, eval, evaluation
Research results, new releases and engineering deep dives that change what is possible or
cheaper. Especially: model releases, inference cost reductions, database internals, systems
performance work, and well-run evaluations.

### Business Insights (weight: 0.9)
keywords: startup, founder, arr, revenue, pricing, growth, go-to-market, fundraising, margins, capex, investor, acquisition, playbook, 4-day work week, productivity, hiring
Concrete lessons about building and running a company: pricing experiments, growth
channels with numbers, capital allocation, org design, and market shifts that affect
developer-tool and infrastructure businesses.

### Developer Tooling (weight: 0.8)
keywords: devtools, cli, docker, ci, build, testing, framework, library, sdk, api, open-sourced, github
New tools and workflows that would save my team real time this quarter.

### Good Vibes (weight: 0.7)
keywords: wholesome, heartwarming, rescue, kindness, funny, comedy, craft, woodworking, nature, sunrise, community, volunteers
Feel-good, funny and calming posts (mostly Instagram and Facebook) for when I'm not in work
mode. These rarely make the work digest but they are what a "happy" or "tired" session is for.

### Saved for Later (weight: 0.6)
Long reads, primers and reference material worth a focused sitting rather than a skim.

## Avoid

- celebrity and gadget consumer news
- affiliate listicles and deals
- keyboard, desk and productivity-gear recommendations
- tabs vs spaces style flame wars
